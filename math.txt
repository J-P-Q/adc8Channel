AT328p @ 16MHz:
- 16MHz / 128 adc prescaler = 125kHz
- 13cycles per conversion / 125kHz = 104us
- 104us per channel conversion time
- 1ms allocated for a frame (1 frame = 8 words)

1000us - (104 * 8)us = 168us remaining for uart before next adc read

uart packet [1 start][8 data][1 stop] = 10 bits
10 * 2 * 8 = 160bit
REQUIREMENT: 160bits/168us = 952k Baud Baud 
Uses 1Mbps


PIC16F877a @ 20MHz
- minimum acceotable is 1.6us of T_AD so prescaler = 32 is the min. 
- 11 T_AD cycles per channel conversion consider as 13 anyway to be safe
- acquisition time = 19.72us ~ 20us  
- 1.6us * 11 = 17.6us per channel | 1.6us * 13 = 20.8us
- 1000us - (8 * (17.6us + 20us)) = 699.2us | 1000us - (8 * (20us + 20.8us)) = 673.6us

REQUIREMENT: 160bits/ 699.2us = 229kk Baud | 160bits/ 673.6us = 238k Baud
